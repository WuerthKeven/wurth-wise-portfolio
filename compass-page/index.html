Built for search.

Every page the AI builds follows the same SEO rules, so the HTML is ready for search engines and AI assistants when it reaches FirstSpirit.

  • One H1 with the main search word. Headings run in order: H1, H2, H3, no level skipped.
  • An SEO title of 30 to 60 characters and a meta description of 120 to 160.
  • The main search word in the title, the H1 and the first 100 words.
  • The direct answer first in each section, so search engines and AI assistants can quote it.
  • Clear structure: comparisons as tables, steps as numbered lists, questions and answers as an FAQ.
  • Alt text that describes every picture.
  • Internal links to the Würth pages and eShop products the page names.
  • Checked after the build: title length, H1 count, heading order, alt text and length. Compass shows what to fix. The checks advise; they never block.
  • No invented facts to fill a block. Short content makes a short page.

What Compass
builds.

Four of the eleven layouts as finished pages. Each one is built for search: one H1, headings in order and a direct answer at the top of every section.

Questions.

Who is it for?

Würth country marketing teams. Thailand is the first market.

Where do the figures come from?

From the team's own Google Search Console and Google Analytics, from recorded AI answers and from the networks' own counts. A figure that was not fetched reads “DATA NOT FETCHED”. It is never shown as zero.

What does the AI do?

It drafts the work on a card and builds HTML pages in the Würth design, in one of eleven layouts, ready for FirstSpirit. It runs inside the product, and a person reviews each page before it is used.

Does it track competitor prices?

No. Compass holds no competitor prices or promotions and no review text. It records which brands the assistants name, and social shows counts only.
